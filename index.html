🧮 Factory Cycle Calculator Explained

The factory cycle calculator is a spreadsheet tool that helps you find the perfect balance between production and resource consumption. Here's a simple explanation:

  • Input: Enter your current income (metal and energy per second), your factory's production speed, and the unit you're building.
  • Output: The calculator tells you how many factories you can run simultaneously without draining your resources, and the optimal build cycle time.
  • Benefit: This prevents "metal overflow" – a situation where you're producing more metal than you can store or use, wasting valuable resources.
